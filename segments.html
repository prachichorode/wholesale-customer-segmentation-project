SEGMENTATION METHODOLOGY

How are business segments created?

The application first performs K-Means clustering. It then calculates the average annual spending of every cluster using the six spending features: Fresh, Milk, Grocery, Frozen, Detergents_Paper and Delicatessen. Clusters are ranked from highest to lowest average spending, and business segment names are assigned according to that ranking.
