MODEL SELECTION LOGIC

How Best K Is Selected

Two evaluation measures are used to understand clustering quality.

📉

Inertia

Inertia measures the sum of squared distances between customers and their assigned cluster centers.

Lower inertia indicates more compact clusters.
📐

Silhouette Score

Silhouette Score measures how well customers fit inside their assigned clusters compared with other clusters.

Higher score indicates better cluster separation.
🎯

Best K

The application selects the K value with the highest Silhouette Score.
