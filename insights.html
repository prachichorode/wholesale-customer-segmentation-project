Segmentation Methodology

Business segment names are not assigned from the numerical cluster ID. The clusters are ranked according to their average annual spending and then mapped to business segments.

Therefore, Cluster 0 does not automatically mean High Value, and Cluster 1 does not automatically mean Low Value.
